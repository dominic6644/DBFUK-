About Dirt Bike Finder UK

Bringing the UK Off-Road Motorcycle Community Together. Welcome to Dirt Bike Finder UK, an independent online platform dedicated to the world of off-road motorcycling. Whether you're searching for your next trials bike, exploring the latest enduro machinery or keeping up with the competitive motocross scene, our aim is to make finding information, discovering motorcycles and following the sport easier. We bring together motorcycle listings from dealers across the United Kingdom, giving riders a convenient place to explore different manufacturers, models and prices without having to visit multiple websites individually. Alongside our motorcycle listings, we publish news, features and industry updates covering the ever-evolving world of off-road motorcycling.

Helping Riders Find Their Next Motorcycle

Finding the right off-road motorcycle can be a challenge. With so many manufacturers, models, engine sizes and riding disciplines to consider, knowing where to start isn't always straightforward. Dirt Bike Finder UK aims to simplify that process. Our platform brings together listings from motorcycle dealerships across the UK, covering a range of off-road disciplines, including:

  • - Trials:Discover specialist trials motorcycles from leading manufacturers, including Beta, GasGas, Montesa, Sherco, Scorpa, TRS and Vertigo.
  • - Enduro: Explore two-stroke and four-stroke enduro motorcycles designed for everything from recreational trail riding to competitive racing.
  • - Motocross: Find motocross machinery from manufacturers across the industry, covering different engine capacities and riding requirements.
  • -Mountain Bikes: Explore our dedicated mountain bike section.

Our aim is to give riders a useful starting point when comparing motorcycles and discovering what's available from dealerships around the country. Dirt Bike Finder UK is a discovery platform rather than a motorcycle retailer. Listings are provided to help visitors find motorcycles advertised by dealerships. Availability, specifications and advertised prices should always be confirmed directly with the relevant seller.

Off-Road Motorcycle News

Our interest in off-road motorcycling extends beyond buying and selling motorcycles. We also cover the latest developments from the world of trials, enduro, motocross, supercross and hard enduro, helping readers stay informed about the sports, manufacturers and riders they follow. Our editorial coverage includes:

  • - New motorcycle launches and manufacturer announcements.
  • - Trials, EnduroGP and motocross championship news.
  • - Supercross and national racing updates.
  • - Hard enduro events and competition results.
  • - Rider interviews and career developments.
  • - Motorcycle technology, equipment and industry developments.
  • -Event previews, race reports and championship standings.

We aim to publish informative, accessible articles that explain what's happening in the sport and why it matters to riders and enthusiasts. Where possible, we use official championship announcements, manufacturer information, race organisers and other credible industry sources to establish the facts behind our reporting.

Approach to Accuracy and Transparency

We believe readers should be able to understand where information comes from and how it is presented. We aim to ensure our articles are accurate, relevant and useful, and we make efforts to distinguish confirmed information from rumours, speculation and unconfirmed announcements. When reporting on race events, we aim to use official results and championship information wherever available. Motorcycle specifications, dealership listings and advertised prices can change. We therefore encourage visitors to confirm important details directly with manufacturers or dealers before making purchasing decisions. If we identify an error in an article or receive credible information that something needs correcting, we aim to review and update the relevant content. You can find more information about how we research, produce and maintain our articles in our Editorial Policy.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to support the UK off-road motorcycle community by making motorcycle discovery and industry news more accessible. Whether you're an experienced competitor, a weekend trail rider, a trials enthusiast or someone researching their first off-road motorcycle, we want Dirt Bike Finder UK to be a useful destination. Thank you for visiting and being part of the off-road motorcycle community.

Get in Touch

We welcome feedback, news tips, corrections, industry announcements and general enquiries. If you're a motorcycle dealer, manufacturer, rider or member of the off-road community, we'd be pleased to hear from you. Visit our Contact Us page to get in touch with Dirt Bike Finder UK.
