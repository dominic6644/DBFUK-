Our Editorial Commitment

At Dirt Bike Finder UK, we are committed to publishing informative, accurate and relevant content for the off-road motorcycle community. Our editorial coverage focuses on trials, enduro, motocross, supercross, hard enduro and the wider motorcycle industry. We understand that readers rely on our website for information about motorcycle releases, championship results, rider developments and industry announcements. Our aim is to provide content that is useful, clearly presented and based on reliable information. This Editorial Policy explains how we research, produce, review and update our articles.

1. Editorial Independence

Dirt Bike Finder UK aims to maintain editorial independence when producing news, features and industry coverage. Our editorial content is intended to inform readers rather than promote a particular manufacturer, motorcycle dealership, rider or product. Editorial decisions, including which stories we cover and how they are presented, are based on their relevance and interest to the off-road motorcycle community. Where content is sponsored, commercially supported or influenced by a commercial relationship, we aim to make this clear to readers. Commercial relationships should not determine the factual conclusions presented in independent editorial reporting.

2. Research and Information Sources

We aim to base our reporting on reliable and relevant sources. Depending on the subject, these may include:

      - Official championship websites and governing bodies.
      - Motorcycle manufacturers and their official announcements.
      - Race organisers and event promoters.
      - Official race results and championship standings.
      - Rider and team announcements.
      - Established motorcycle publications and industry news sources.
      - Interviews, statements and information provided directly by relevant individuals or organisations.

When covering competitive events, we aim to verify race results, rider positions and championship points using official information wherever possible. When reporting on upcoming motorcycle releases, specifications or industry developments, we aim to distinguish confirmed announcements from rumours and speculation. We do not intentionally present unconfirmed information as established fact.

3. Article Creation and Originality

We aim to produce original articles that provide readers with useful context and information. Our articles may cover breaking news, race reports, championship developments, motorcycle launches, previews, reviews and industry features. When information originates from manufacturer announcements, official press releases or other publications, we aim to rewrite and present the material in our own words rather than reproduce another publisher's work. Where appropriate, we add additional context, explain the significance of developments and provide relevant information to help readers understand a story. We may use digital tools, including artificial intelligence, to assist with research, organisation, drafting or editing. However, published material remains subject to editorial oversight, factual checking and review. AI-generated material is not treated as an independent source of verification. We do not intend to publish automatically generated material without meaningful editorial review.

4. Accuracy and Fact Checking

Accuracy is an important part of our editorial process. Before publishing, we aim to check important details, including:

  • - Rider names and team affiliations.
  • - Race results and championship positions.
  • - Event dates and locations.
  • - Motorcycle model names and specifications.
  • - Manufacturer announcements.
  • - Relevant quotes and attributed statements.

Where information is uncertain or has not been officially confirmed, we aim to communicate that clearly. If reliable new information becomes available after publication, we may update an article to reflect the latest developments.

5. Corrections and Article Updates

Despite our efforts, mistakes can occasionally happen. If an article contains an inaccurate result, incorrect specification, outdated information or another factual error, we welcome readers bringing it to our attention. When a correction is necessary, we aim to:

  • - Investigate the information brought to our attention.
  • - Check the relevant details against reliable sources.
  • -Correct the article where an error is confirmed.
  • - Update information when significant new developments occur.

We do not intentionally retain information that we know to be factually incorrect. Readers can submit correction requests through our Contact Us page.

6. Authors and Editorial Responsibility

Articles published on Dirt Bike Finder UK are attributed to their respective authors or editorial contributors. Author information is intended to help readers understand who is responsible for producing the content. Where author biographies are available, they may provide additional information about the contributor's interests, background and areas of coverage. We aim to ensure that author information is accurate and that contributors are not presented as having qualifications, experience or professional credentials they do not possess.

7. Motorcycle Listings and Commercial Information

Dirt Bike Finder UK also provides a motorcycle discovery platform featuring listings advertised by dealerships. These listings help visitors discover motorcycles available through retailers across the United Kingdom. We are not the seller of the motorcycles displayed on the website unless explicitly stated otherwise. Listing information, including prices, specifications, mileage and availability, may change without notice. We encourage visitors to contact the relevant dealership directly to confirm the details of a motorcycle before travelling, making a purchase or placing a deposit. The inclusion of a motorcycle or dealership on our platform does not automatically represent an endorsement or independent assessment of that seller.

8. Reviews, Recommendations and Product Coverage

When covering motorcycles, equipment or related products, we aim to provide balanced and informative information. Readers should be able to understand the difference between manufacturer-provided specifications, independent reporting, personal experience and formal product testing. We will not describe a motorcycle or product as independently tested unless genuine testing has taken place. Where information is supplied by a manufacturer, dealer or other commercial source, we aim to make the nature of that information clear.

9. Advertising and Sponsored Content

Dirt Bike Finder UK may display advertising to support the operation and maintenance of the website. Advertising is separate from our independent editorial coverage. Where sponsored articles, paid partnerships, affiliate links or other commercially influenced content are published, we aim to provide clear disclosure so readers can identify commercial material. Advertising does not automatically represent an editorial recommendation or endorsement of a particular product, dealership or manufacturer.

10. Images, Copyright and Attribution

We aim to respect copyright and intellectual property rights when publishing photographs, graphics and other media. Images may originate from manufacturers, event organisers, official teams, press materials or other authorised sources. Where appropriate, image credits and acknowledgements are provided. If you believe material published on Dirt Bike Finder UK infringes your copyright or has been incorrectly attributed, please contact us with details s o the matter can be investigated.

11. Feedback and Contact

we value feedback from readers, riders, manufacturers, dealerships, teams and members of the wider off-road motorcycle community. If you have a correction, news tip, industry announcement or editorial enquiry, please contact us through our Contact Us page. We aim to review genuine enquiries and make appropriate changes where necessary.

Our Commitment

objective is to build a reliable and useful resource for the UK off-road motorcycle community. We are committed to improving our content, maintaining transparency and providing readers with information they can use and understand.
